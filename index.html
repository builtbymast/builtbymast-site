What usually breaks

Three things I see again and again.

These are the issues that come up most often when I review local business sites. Each one is fixable, and none of them needs a huge budget.

01

People can’t find how to contact you on a phone.

Problem

The number is buried in the footer, the form is three taps deep, or the button is too small to hit with a thumb. The visitor gives up and calls someone else.

How I fix it

Tap-to-call and a short enquiry form sit right where people look, on every page, sized for thumbs.

02

Pages are slow on mobile data.

Problem

Oversized photos, heavy page builders and cheap hosting add up. On a phone in a car park, a blank screen for a few seconds is enough to lose someone.

How I fix it

Properly sized images, a clean, lightweight build and reliable hosting on your own account.

03

It’s not clear what you do, or what to do next.

Problem

Vague headlines, stock photos and no obvious next step. Visitors can’t tell in a glance whether you’re the right fit, so they leave.

How I fix it

A plain-English headline, the service area up front and one clear next step on every page.
